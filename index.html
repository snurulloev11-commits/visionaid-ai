WHY IT MATTERS

Accessibility should be built into technology.

VisionAid AI is a lightweight prototype exploring how computer vision and speech output can turn visual information into a more accessible experience. It is designed as an assistive aid, not a replacement for mobility tools or professional accessibility technology.
